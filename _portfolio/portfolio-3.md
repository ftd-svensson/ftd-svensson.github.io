Jurassic Gainz is a single-player arena shooter where you must defeat dinosaurs to get back your eggs for bulking! The whole program was built from scratch in 6 weeks and functioned as our first game project at BTH.
For this project, we worked in a team of 5 developers, simulating a SCRUM work environment while building the entire game engine and game logic from scratch.

My main areas of responsibility in this project were integrating the FMOD audio solution into our game engine, setting up the rendering passes and shadow mapping, and developing the collision and enemy movement systems.
